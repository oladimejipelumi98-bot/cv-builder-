How to use

1. Tap "Fill with sample CV" or type your own details.
2. Pick a style, colour and font.
3. Add your photo if you want.
4. Tap "Download CV (PDF)".
5. Open the Cover letter tab to make a matching letter.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. You can make and download as many CVs as you like.

Is my information safe?

Your details stay on your own phone. They are saved in your browser and are not sent to any server.

My download did not start

Open this page in Chrome, check your internet connection, and try again.

Can I keep more than one CV?

Yes. Use the "Saved CV" box at the top to switch between CV 1, CV 2 and CV 3.

Can I add this page to my home screen?

Yes. In Chrome, tap the menu (three dots) and choose "Add to Home screen".
